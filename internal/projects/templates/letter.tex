{\large\bfseries\color{accent}\lettersubject}

Dear Recipient,

Open with the purpose of your letter in a clear sentence. If you are responding
to a vacancy, proposal or earlier conversation, identify it here and explain
why you are writing to this recipient.

Develop the main point with a specific example. For an application, connect
your relevant experience to the organization's needs. For a formal request,
provide the context, the supporting information and the outcome you seek.
Keep the focus on what the recipient needs to make a decision.

Add a second supporting paragraph if needed. Use concrete evidence rather than
general claims, and keep each paragraph focused on one idea. Mention any
enclosed documents and explain how they support your request.

Close with a practical next step: suggest a meeting, ask for a response by an
appropriate date or explain how the recipient can contact you. Thank them for
their time and consideration.
